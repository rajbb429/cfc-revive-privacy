    About 4-7-6-6

    Each breath takes 23 seconds: breathe in for 4, hold for 7, breathe out for 6, and rest empty for 6. That slows you to about 2.6 breaths a minute, well below the usual 12 to 20.

    in 4hold 7out 6hold 6

    How to do it

    • Sit or lie comfortably. Let your shoulders drop.
    • Breathe in quietly through your nose, into your belly.
    • Hold softly. Keep your throat relaxed rather than clamped.
    • Breathe out slowly through your mouth, as if through a straw.
    • Rest with empty lungs until the next breath begins.
    • Close your eyes if you like. The bowl or tone marks each change, so you don't need to watch.

    Go gently

    The holds can feel long at first. If you feel dizzy, lightheaded or short of breath, return to normal breathing. Talk to a doctor before breath-holding practice if you are pregnant or have a heart or lung condition. This app is for relaxation and is not medical advice.
