Privacy Policy for Stillwater

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Overview

Stillwater ("the App") is a guided breathing app offering 4-7-6-6 breathing and, with a one-time unlock, guided Valsalva and Müller maneuvers and resisted breathing exercises. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party.

Specifically, the App:

  • Does not require account creation or login
  • Does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • Does not use cookies for tracking
  • Does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, or crash reports

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves your settings, your session history (date, length, and number of breaths or rounds), whether you have read the maneuver safety notes, and whether you own the full-app unlock. This data is stored only in your device's local browser storage and never leaves your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it.

The App may keep your screen awake during a session and may vibrate your device at each phase change. Voice guidance uses your device's built-in speech engine.

Purchases

The optional full-app unlock is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; the App never sees your payment details. The App asks Google Play only whether your account owns the unlock. To confirm a purchase, the App may send the purchase token issued by Google Play to the developer's verification server, which checks it with Google and keeps no other information about you.

Network Access

The App loads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time it opens and then works offline. Google Fonts may receive your IP address as part of that request, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Health Information

The App does not ask for or record any health information. The Valsalva and Müller maneuvers and resisted breathing exercises are guided timers only and are not medical advice.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.
