Stress to Strong is a calm, uncluttered breathing app in two parts. First, slow your breath down to let stress settle. Then, when you're ready, build a stronger breath: longer breath holds, a more mobile ribcage and better pressure control through your core.

Open the app, tap anywhere, and follow a quiet drawing of your ribs as it widens on the in-breath and settles on the out-breath. Or close your eyes and let soft sounds, a calm voice and gentle vibration keep the rhythm for you. The controls stay out of the way until you touch the screen.

TO CALM (FREE)
• 4-7-8 breathing: in for 4, hold for 7, out for 8. The relaxing breath for winding down and falling asleep.
• Cyclic sighing: two breaths in through the nose, a long one and a short top-up, then a long, slow breath out. Also known as the physiological sigh.
• Long exhale: in for 4, out for 8. The simplest way to slow down, with no holds.
• 4-7-6-6 breathing (4766): in for 4, hold for 7, out for 6, rest for 6. Slows you to about 2.6 breaths a minute.
• Equal breathing: in for 5, out for 5, six steady breaths a minute.
• Box breathing: in, hold, out and hold for 4 each, to stay steady under pressure.
• Make your own: set your own in, hold, out and hold counts, name the breath and keep it in your list.

TO BUILD (FREE)
• Breath-hold practice: calm holds after a normal breath that grow a few seconds each time a session feels easy, with a Breathe now button whenever you need it.
• Hold check: measure your comfortable breath hold and start your practice at half of it.

FULL APP (ONE-TIME UNLOCK)
• Resisted breathing: slow 12-second breaths through a pinched nose or a pinhole in your fist, no equipment needed
• Belt breaths: a belt snug around your upper ribcage, then breaths into it with arm raises, twists and side reaches, for ribcage expansion and midline control
• Valsalva maneuver: breathe in, bear down, recover, with 5, 10 or 15 second effort and a 3-2-1 countdown
• Müller maneuver: breathe out, pull in against a closed airway, recover
• Bunny breathing: six quick sniffs in and six quick bursts out through the nose, to wake up and refocus
• Yahweh breath prayer: a slow, contemplative breath prayer through the nose, feeling "Yah" on the breath in and "weh" on the breath out
• Advanced progressions: head up, down, left and right built into 4-7-6-6, Valsalva, Müller, resisted breathing and belt breaths
• The voice guide talks you through every step, so you never need to look at the screen
• Every exercise added later, at no extra cost
